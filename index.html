Common questions

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Preload has no accounts and no server, so I can't see, access or recover your records.

What happens if I delete the app or change phones?

Deleting the app deletes its data. If you set up a new phone from a backup of your old one (iCloud on iPhone, Google backup on Android), your records should come with it. To keep your own copy of a bike's history, use the Export tab to save it as a PDF.

I'm not getting reminders

Check that notifications are allowed for Preload in your phone's Settings. Reminders are sent for date-based items, such as time intervals and renewals. Items due by distance appear in the app instead.
